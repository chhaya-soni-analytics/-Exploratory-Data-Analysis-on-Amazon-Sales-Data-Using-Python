📊 Exploratory Data Analysis on Amazon Sales Data (Python)

Author: Chhaya Soni
Role: Aspiring Data Analyst / Data Scientist

🔍 Project Overview

In today’s competitive e-commerce landscape, understanding customer purchasing behavior is critical for business success.
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Amazon sales data to uncover:

Sales distribution patterns

Category-wise performance

Pricing variability and outliers

Geographic sales trends

Fulfilment and logistics efficiency

The analysis is conducted using Python in Google Colab, with visualizations to support data-driven retail decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze transaction amount distribution

Identify top-selling product categories

Detect high-value pricing outliers

Understand geographic sales behavior

Compare B2B vs B2C transactions

Evaluate fulfilment and courier performance

🧰 Tools & Technologies

Python

Pandas & NumPy – Data manipulation

Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization

Google Colab – Notebook environment

📁 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains product-level Amazon sales information including:

Order ID

Product Category

Quantity

Transaction Amount

Shipping state & postal code

Fulfilment method

Courier status

B2B indicator

⚠️ This is a simulated dataset designed to represent real-world Amazon sales patterns.

📊 Key Analysis & Visualizations
1️⃣ Transaction Amount Distribution

Most purchases fall between ₹500–₹1000, indicating dominance of low-to-mid priced products.

(Histogram visualization included in notebook)

2️⃣ High-Value Outliers Detection

Transactions above ₹5000 were identified using boxplot analysis.
These outliers can significantly distort average-based metrics.

3️⃣ Category-Wise Pricing Variability

Categories such as T-shirts and Blazers show wide price dispersion, suggesting mixed product positioning and pricing strategies.

4️⃣ Top Product Categories by Sales Frequency

T-shirts and Shirts dominate total order volume

Categories like Shoes, Watches, and Wallets show lower sales frequency

5️⃣ Geographic Sales Analysis

Metro and tier-1 states contribute the highest number of orders

Maharashtra and Karnataka lead in total transactions

This insight supports state-level marketing and logistics planning.

6️⃣ B2B vs B2C Analysis

The platform is predominantly B2C-driven

B2B transactions represent a potential growth opportunity through bulk pricing strategies

7️⃣ Order Status & Fulfilment Analysis

Amazon-fulfilled orders show higher shipment success rates

Merchant-fulfilled orders experience higher cancellation rates

8️⃣ Courier & Logistics Performance

Easy Ship deliveries perform better operationally

Courier delays strongly correlate with order cancellations

🔑 Key Insights

Apparel categories drive the majority of sales volume

Sales amounts exhibit a right-skewed distribution

High-value outliers disproportionately impact revenue

Pricing inconsistencies exist within the same category

Amazon fulfilment outperforms merchant fulfilment

Faster shipping reduces cancellation risk

Sales are concentrated in urban and tier-1 regions

The platform remains heavily consumer-focused (B2C)

📌 Business Recommendations

Standardize pricing within high-variance categories

Promote underperforming product segments

Segment high-value transactions for revenue optimization

Improve merchant fulfilment reliability

Build category-wise dashboards for real-time monitoring
